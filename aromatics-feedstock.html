Aromatics & Feedstock

Heavy Alkylate

Byproduct of linear alkylbenzene production used as a thermal fluid, in lube oil blending, and specialized industrial solvents.

Raffinate

Petrochemical stream remaining after extraction processes, heavily utilized as a high-grade solvent and chemical blending feedstock.

Pyrolysis Gasoline (Pygas)

Complex hydrocarbon mixture produced during steam cracking, prized as a rich source for benzene, toluene, and xylene recovery.

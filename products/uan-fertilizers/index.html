Liquid Urea Ammonium Nitrate (UAN) Fertilizers

HS Code: 310280

Urea Ammonium Nitrate (UAN) solution is a liquid mixture of urea and ammonium nitrate, recognized as one of the most efficient nitrogen sources for modern agriculture. Due to its high absorption rate and compatibility with advanced irrigation systems, it plays a vital role in increasing farm productivity. SATCO supplies this product with high purity and optimal chemical stability for industrial and agricultural use internationally.

Product Grades & Variations

UAN is supplied in various concentrations, classified by total nitrogen percentage:

  • UAN 28%: Standard formulation for temperate climates.
  • UAN 32%: High-concentration grade for maximum efficiency in large-scale agricultural operations.

Key Advantages

  • Multi-Stage Nutrition: Provides both fast-acting nitrogen (nitrate) and gradual-release nitrogen (urea) simultaneously.
  • Ease of Application: Easily miscible with other liquid fertilizers and agricultural chemicals to reduce operational costs.
  • High Precision: Uniform distribution in the field due to its liquid nature, leading to reduced fertilizer waste.
  • Handling Safety: High chemical stability and non-flammability under standard storage conditions.

Primary Applications

  • Fertigation: Ideal for use in pressurized and drip irrigation systems.
  • Foliar Spray: Easily absorbed through leaves to immediately address nitrogen deficiencies.
  • Conservation Agriculture: Suitable for modern tillage methods requiring precise fertilizer placement.

Packaging & Supply

Maintaining product integrity during maritime and inland transportation:

  • ISO Tanks: The standard for the safe transport of bulk liquid chemicals.
  • Drums & IBCs: Flexible options for smaller scales and regional distribution.

Polyethylene Terephthalate (PET): Clarity, Strength, and Safety in Packaging Industries

HS Code: 390761

Polyethylene Terephthalate (PET) is a thermoplastic polymer from the polyester family. Due to its unique characteristics, such as high clarity, excellent mechanical strength, and low gas permeability, it has become the first choice for the packaging industry. At SATCO, PET grades with controlled Intrinsic Viscosity (IV) are supplied for the production of containers and industrial fibers.

Product Grades & Variations

  • Bottle Grade: Features appropriate viscosity for producing various beverage bottles, mineral water, edible oils, and detergents.
  • Fiber Grade: Ideal for producing synthetic fibers (polyester) for use in the textile industry, carpet weaving, and industrial fabric production.
  • Sheet Grade: Suitable for producing transparent blister packaging sheets and specialty disposable containers.

Key Advantages

  • Excellent Clarity: A glass-like, brilliant appearance that doubles the appeal of the final product.
  • Strong Barrier Properties: High resistance to oxygen and carbon dioxide permeation, which increases the shelf life of food and beverages.
  • High Mechanical Strength: Excellent impact and pressure resistance, allowing for the production of lightweight and thin-walled containers.
  • Recyclability: Full compatibility with recycling processes and conversion back into raw material (100% recyclable).

Primary Applications

  • Beverage Industry: Production of bottles for mineral water, soft drinks, juices, and carbonated beverages.
  • Food Industry: Packaging for edible oils, sauces, spices, and dried fruits.
  • Textiles & Apparel: Production of polyester fibers for clothing, curtains, furniture, and carpets.
  • Medical Equipment: Production of pharmaceutical packaging containers and laboratory equipment.

Packaging & Supply

Maintaining quality and resin dehumidification to prevent production process defects:

  • 25 kg Bags: Standard for transport and easy handling.
  • Jumbo Bags (1 Ton): Economical for large manufacturers and fully automated production lines.

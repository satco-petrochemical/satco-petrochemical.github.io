Chemical Polypropylene (PP Chemical): Thermal Resistance and Stability in Industrial Conditions

HS Code: 390210

Chemical Polypropylene (PP Chemical), often referred to as Impact Copolymers, is designed for applications requiring high impact resistance, even at low temperatures[cite: 6]. At SATCO, this grade is produced and supplied with an optimized formulation to achieve the ideal balance between mechanical strength and chemical stability[cite: 6].

Product Grades & Variations

The diversity of chemical grades allows for meeting the needs of specialized industries[cite: 6]:

  • Injection Molding Grade: Ideal for producing automotive parts, toolboxes, industrial containers, and home appliances subject to physical stress[cite: 6].
  • Blow Molding Grade: Suitable for producing chemical storage containers, hollow parts, and industrial fluid transfer systems[cite: 6].
  • Pipe and Fitting Grade: Designed for producing sewage piping systems and fluid transfer components with high thermal and chemical resistance[cite: 6].

Key Advantages

  • Impact Resistance: Structural stability under harsh operating conditions and resistance to cold-weather breakage[cite: 6].
  • Excellent Chemical Resistance: High compatibility with corrosive chemicals, solvents, and acidic/alkaline environments[cite: 6].
  • Thermal Stability: Maintenance of mechanical properties across a wide range of operating temperatures[cite: 6].
  • Optimal Processability: Precise formability in complex molds with controlled shrinkage rates[cite: 6].

Primary Applications

  • Automotive Industry: Production of dashboards, bumpers, battery cases, and under-the-hood components[cite: 6].
  • Home Appliances: Washing machine and dishwasher bodies, and refrigerator interior parts[cite: 6].
  • Industrial Equipment: Chemical storage tanks, pipe fittings, and secure packaging containers[cite: 6].
  • Electronics Industry: Durable plastic components for electrical equipment[cite: 6].

Packaging & Supply

Maintaining quality during the supply process[cite: 6]:

  • 25 kg Bags: Industrial standard for protection against environmental contaminants[cite: 6].
  • Jumbo Bags (1 Ton): Suitable for bulk storage and efficient handling in production lines[cite: 6].

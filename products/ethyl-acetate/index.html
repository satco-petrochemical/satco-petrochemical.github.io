Ethyl Acetate: A Versatile Solvent for Paint, Resin, and Packaging Industries

HS Code: 291531

Ethyl Acetate, an ester with a pleasant fruity odor and excellent solubility, is one of the most widely used organic solvents in the industry. It holds a significant position due to its optimal volatility and lower toxicity compared to many other solvents. At SATCO, this product is supplied with the highest purity for sensitive industrial and commercial applications.

Grades and Product Variety

  • Industrial Grade: Featuring high purity, suitable for use in the production of paints, lacquers, resins, printing inks, and industrial thinners.
  • Pharma/Food Grade: Featuring minimal impurities, intended for use in the extraction of active pharmaceutical ingredients, flavorings, and sensitive packaging processes.

Key Advantages

  • Optimal Evaporation Rate: Controlled volatility, ensuring fast and uniform drying of final products.
  • High Solvency Power: Exceptional ability to dissolve nitrocellulose, acrylic resins, and a wide range of polymers.
  • Chemical Stability: Consistent quality maintained across various environmental conditions.
  • Safety Standards: Adherence to strict packaging standards to prevent evaporation and ensure safety during transportation.

Primary Applications

  • Paint and Coating Industry: A primary solvent in the production of lacquers, cellulosic paints, and protective coatings.
  • Printing Inks: Used as a fast-drying solvent in flexographic and gravure printing inks.
  • Pharmaceutical Industry: A solvent in purification processes, drug crystallization, and antibiotic production.
  • Food Industry: Used as a flavoring agent and a solvent in extraction processes and packaging.

Packaging and Supply

  • Steel Drums (180 kg): Industrial standard for safe and long-term storage.
  • ISO Tank and Bulk: Designed for large-scale requirements and continuous production lines.

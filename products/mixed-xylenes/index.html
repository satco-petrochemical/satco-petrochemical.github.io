Mixed Xylenes: Advanced Industrial Solvent and Petrochemical Intermediate

HS Code: 290244

Mixed Xylenes consist of a mixture of three structural isomers (ortho, meta, and para-xylene) along with ethylbenzene, supplied as a clear, colorless liquid with a characteristic odor. At SATCO, this product is supplied with high quality and in accordance with refinery standards for industrial and solvent applications.

Grades and Product Variety

  • Industrial/Solvent Grade: Suitable for coating applications, paint manufacturing, and formulated solvents.
  • Petrochemical Grade: With suitable purity for isomer separation and chemical syntheses.

Key Advantages

  • High Solvency Power: Ability to dissolve a wide range of resins, polymers, fats, and oils.
  • Balanced Evaporation Rate: Consistent drying time in paint and coating formulations.
  • Industrial Reactivity: Application as a key raw material in the production of phthalic acid and terephthalic acid.

Primary Applications

  • Paint and Resin Industries: Used as the primary solvent in thinners, industrial paints, and surface coatings.
  • Adhesive and Ink Industries: Improving viscosity and drying speed in adhesive and printing ink formulations.
  • Chemical Production: Raw material for producing isophthalic acid, terephthalic acid (for polyester), and phthalic anhydride.
  • Petrochemical and Refining Industries: Enhancing fuel octane number and utilization in extraction processes.

Packaging and Supply

  • Specialized Metal Drums (200L): Standard, durable, and safe for the transport of industrial liquids.
  • ISO Tank and Bulk: Suitable for major consumers and manufacturing complexes.

Raffinate: Valuable Solvent and Feedstock in the Petrochemical Industry

HS Code: 271012

Raffinate is a byproduct resulting from the solvent extraction process in petroleum and petrochemical refineries, obtained after the separation of aromatics. This hydrocarbon compound is supplied as a clear, colorless liquid. At SATCO, raffinate is supplied with standard technical specifications for industrial and solvent applications.

Grades and Product Variety

  • Industrial/Solvent Grade: Suitable for formulation applications, adhesive manufacturing, and industrial cleaning.
  • Petrochemical Feedstock Grade: Containing paraffins and cycloparaffins for cracking units.

Key Advantages

  • Low Aromatic Content: Greater safety in solvent applications and reduced environmental and personal hazards.
  • Good Chemical Stability: High resistance to changes in industrial formulations.
  • Optimal Evaporation Rate: Appropriate drying speed in coating and adhesive processes.

Primary Applications

  • Adhesive and Rubber Industries: Used as a solvent in the production of various industrial adhesives and tire manufacturing.
  • Paint and Resin Industries: Application in thinners and low-odor diluting solvents.
  • Cracking Unit Feedstock: Used as a raw material in ethylene production and light hydrocarbon manufacturing.
  • Industrial Cleaning: Application as a strong degreaser for industrial parts.

Packaging and Supply

  • Specialized Metal Drums (200L): Standard and safe for warehousing and distribution.
  • ISO Tank and Bulk: Suitable for economical delivery to large industries.

High Impact Polystyrene (HIPS): High Impact Strength and Structural Rigidity in Manufacturing Industries

HS Code: 390319

High Impact Polystyrene (HIPS), produced by incorporating polybutadiene rubber into the polystyrene structure, is designed for applications requiring high impact resistance alongside ease of processing. At SATCO, this grade is supplied with standardized technical specifications as a cost-effective alternative to high-priced engineering plastics.

Product Grades & Variations

The diversity in HIPS compositions enables use across various industries:

  • Injection Moulding Grade: Ideal for producing parts with complex geometries that require high impact strength (e.g., home appliance housings).
  • Extrusion (Sheet) Grade: Suitable for producing thermoformed sheets for industrial packaging and dairy containers.
  • Flame Retardant Grade: Designed for electronic equipment requiring fire safety standards.

Key Advantages

  • Excellent Impact Resistance: Significant reduction in the probability of breakage during transport and daily use.
  • Ease of Processing: Rapid formability in injection molds and extruders with low shrinkage.
  • Matte and Attractive Appearance: A surface suitable for printing and labeling, ideal for retail packaging.
  • Structural Stability: Maintenance of mechanical properties over time and resistance to deformation under stress.

Primary Applications

  • Home Appliances: Production of vacuum cleaner housings, refrigerators, air conditioners, and washing machines.
  • Packaging Industry: Dairy containers (yogurt and butter tubs), food storage trays, and pharmaceutical packaging.
  • Office & Electronic Equipment: Printer housings, computer cases, telephones, and office gear.
  • Construction & Advertising: Production of advertising signs, plastic modeling, and decorative parts.

Packaging & Supply

Ensuring quality and material purity in the supply chain:

  • 25 kg Bags: Standard for safe storage and moisture prevention.
  • Jumbo Bags (1 Ton): Optimized for transport to high-volume production units.

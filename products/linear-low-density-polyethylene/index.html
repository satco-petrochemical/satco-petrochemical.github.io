Linear Low-Density Polyethylene (LLDPE): High Tensile Strength and Durability in Modern Packaging

HS Code: 390110

Linear Low-Density Polyethylene (LLDPE), due to its linear molecular structure with short branches, offers a combination of low-density polyethylene flexibility and higher structural strength[cite: 4]. This polymer is widely used for producing thin films with excellent tensile and impact resistance[cite: 4]. SATCO supplies specialized LLDPE grades with industrial quality for advanced packaging applications[cite: 4].

Product Grades & Variations

The diversity of LLDPE grades allows for optimization of the production process[cite: 4]:

  • Blown Film Grade: Suitable for producing industrial stretch films, agricultural films, and heavy-duty packaging bags that require high tear resistance[cite: 4].
  • Injection & Rotomolding Grade: Ideal for producing large industrial parts, liquid storage tanks, and plastic toys with high impact strength[cite: 4].
  • Masterbatch Carrier Grade: Designed as a base for uniform distribution of pigments and additives in the plastics industry[cite: 4].

Key Advantages

  • High Mechanical Strength: Excellent resistance to puncture and tearing, even at very low thicknesses[cite: 4].
  • Clarity & Gloss: Attractive and transparent appearance for retail packaging[cite: 4].
  • Thermal Stability: Optimal performance in high-speed production processes and reduced probability of cracking[cite: 4].
  • Blending Capability: Excellent synergy when blended with LDPE to improve the final product's properties[cite: 4].

Primary Applications

  • Packaging Industry: Production of stretch films, multilayer packaging, and heavy-duty transport bags[cite: 4].
  • Agriculture: Production of mulch films and greenhouse covers resistant to weather conditions[cite: 4].
  • Logistics & Transportation: Packaging of industrial pallets to maintain load integrity[cite: 4].
  • Industrial Production: Manufacturing of large parts and tanks that require structural resistance alongside low weight[cite: 4].

Packaging & Supply

Ensuring raw material quality during transit[cite: 4]:

  • 25 kg Bags: Industrial standard for maintaining granule integrity[cite: 4].
  • Jumbo Bags (1 Ton): Suitable for facilitating bulk handling and storage[cite: 4].

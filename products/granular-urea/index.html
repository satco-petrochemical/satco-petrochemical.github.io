Granular Urea

Granular Urea is a high-grade, premium nitrogen fertilizer widely utilized in modern agriculture to maximize crop yields. Characterized by its hard, spherical white granules and excellent storage and handling properties, it ensures a controlled and uniform release of nitrogen to plants, supporting robust vegetative growth across diverse soil and climate conditions.

Key Features & Advantages

  • High Nitrogen Content: Contains 46% nitrogen, making it one of the most concentrated solid nitrogenous fertilizers available.
  • Superior Physical Strength: Hard, uniform granules minimize dust formation and breakage during handling, transport, and mechanical spreading.
  • Excellent Solubility: Completely and rapidly soluble in water, facilitating efficient root absorption and fertigation applications.
  • Low Biuret Content: Manufactured to strict quality standards to ensure safe application without phytotoxic effects on sensitive crops.
  • Storage Stability: Treated to prevent caking, ensuring free-flowing characteristics over extended storage periods.

Primary Applications

  • Agriculture & Crop Nutrition: Serves as a direct soil application fertilizer for cereals, corn, rice, cotton, and various horticultural crops.
  • Blended Fertilizers: Ideal component for blending with bulk fertilizers (like DAP and MOP) to create customized NPK formulations.
  • Industrial Uses: Utilized as a raw material in the manufacturing of resins, glues, plastics, and various chemical syntheses.
  • Environmental Control: Used in selective catalytic reduction (SCR) systems (as DEF/AdBlue solutions) to reduce nitrogen oxide emissions from diesel engines.

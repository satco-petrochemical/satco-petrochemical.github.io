Phosphoric Acid: The Industrial Essential

HS Code: 280920

Phosphoric Acid is a vital mineral acid characterized by its diverse utility across agricultural and food-grade industrial sectors. As a core component in the production of high-performance fertilizers, it also serves as a critical intermediate in chemical manufacturing, metal treatment, and water purification. SATCO supplies high-grade Phosphoric Acid engineered to meet the stringent quality and purity standards required by global industrial partners.

Product Grades & Variations

SATCO ensures a reliable supply of Phosphoric Acid in grades optimized for specific industrial requirements:

  • Technical Grade (85%): The industry standard for industrial applications, widely utilized in metal treatment, chemical synthesis, and industrial cleaning agents.
  • Food Grade (75%-85%): Refined to meet rigorous food safety standards, serving as a critical additive in food and beverage processing, acidity regulation, and additive manufacturing.

Key Advantages

  • High Reactivity: Optimal concentration for maximizing the efficiency of complex chemical reactions in synthesis.
  • Consistent Quality: Sourced to maintain stringent impurity limits, ensuring performance reliability in sensitive applications.
  • Versatility: A critical feedstock for a wide array of agricultural, food, and industrial manufacturing sectors.
  • Supply Chain Safety: Rigorous adherence to international safety standards for the handling and transport of chemical materials.

Primary Applications

  • Fertilizer Industry: A fundamental reagent for the production of DAP (Diammonium Phosphate) and MAP (Monoammonium Phosphate) fertilizers.
  • Food & Beverage: Used as a key acidulant and flavoring agent in beverages and as a preservative in various food products.
  • Metallurgy: Essential for metal phosphating, rust removal, and surface passivation to enhance corrosion resistance.
  • Chemical Manufacturing: A key component in the production of detergents, water treatment chemicals, and fire retardants.

Polycarbonate (PC): Glass-Like Clarity and Exceptional Impact Resistance in Modern Engineering

HS Code: 390740

Polycarbonate (PC) is an advanced engineering polymer recognized for its unique combination of optical clarity, high thermal resistance, and exemplary impact strength[cite: 6]. At SATCO, PC grades are supplied in compliance with global standards for critical and precision applications[cite: 6].

Product Grades & Variations

  • General Purpose Grade: Suitable for producing transparent and rigid parts for general applications[cite: 6].
  • UV-Resistant Grade: Designed for outdoor use requiring stability against sunlight exposure[cite: 6].
  • Flame Retardant Grade: Ideal for electrical equipment and vehicle interior components that must meet strict safety requirements[cite: 6].

Key Advantages

  • Unrivalled Impact Resistance: A shatterproof alternative to glass in high-risk environments[cite: 6].
  • High Clarity: Excellent light transmission comparable to glass with significantly lower weight[cite: 6].
  • Thermal Stability: Maintenance of mechanical properties across a wide temperature range (from extreme cold to high heat)[cite: 6].
  • Electrical Insulation: Excellent performance as an insulator in sensitive electronic components[cite: 6].

Primary Applications

  • Construction Industry: Production of transparent roofing sheets, canopies, greenhouses, and partition walls[cite: 6].
  • Automotive Industry: Production of headlamps, dashboards, and internal body components[cite: 6].
  • Electronics: Computer equipment cases, mobile phone parts, and electrical circuit insulators[cite: 6].
  • Safety Equipment: Bulletproof glass, safety helmets, and face shields[cite: 6].

Packaging & Supply

Ensuring quality and polymer material purity[cite: 6]:

  • 25 kg Bags: Standard for transport and controlled use[cite: 6].
  • Jumbo Bags (1 Ton): Optimized for high-consumption industries and mass production lines[cite: 6].

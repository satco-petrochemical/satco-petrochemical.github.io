Phenol: Vital Chemical in Advanced Industrial Synthesis

HS Code: 290711

Phenol is an aromatic, crystalline compound serving as an essential raw material in the production of a wide range of resins, plastics, synthetic fibers, and pharmaceuticals. At SATCO, phenol is supplied with the highest standards of purity and strict adherence to safety protocols.

Grades and Product Variety

  • Synthesis Grade: High purity, suitable for polymerization processes and industrial resin manufacturing.

Key Advantages

  • High Purity and Quality: Minimum impurities such as water and hydrocarbon compounds to achieve the best reaction yield.
  • Exceptional Reactivity: Stable cyclic structure capable of participating in substitution and condensation reactions.
  • Versatility: Acting as a building block in various petrochemical and pharmaceutical industries.

Primary Applications

  • Phenolic Resins Production: Manufacturing phenol-formaldehyde resins for wood, composite, and brake pad industries.
  • Plastics and Polymer Industries: Producing Bisphenol A (BPA) for polycarbonates and epoxies.
  • Caprolactam Production: A key raw material in nylon fibers and resins.
  • Pharmaceutical and Chemical Industries: Synthesis of antiseptics, aspirin, and herbicides.

Packaging and Supply

  • Specialized Metal Drums (Solid/Flake packaging): Secure and resistant to environmental conditions.
  • ISO Tank and Molten Bulk: For delivery to large industrial complexes.

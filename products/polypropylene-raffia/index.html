Polypropylene Raffia (PP Raffia): High Strength and Efficiency in Plastic Textile Production

HS Code: 390210

Polypropylene Raffia (PP Raffia) is a specialized grade of the polypropylene family that, due to its controlled Melt Flow Index (MFI), provides excellent processability in the production of fibers and tape yarns. At SATCO, this grade is produced and supplied with the aim of achieving maximum tensile strength and dimensional uniformity for weaving industries.

Product Grades & Variations

The diversity of Raffia grades enables the production of a wide range of products:

  • Woven Bag Grade (Standard Raffia): Ideal for producing yarns used in packaging bags for rice, sugar, flour, and animal feed.
  • Strapping Grade: Designed for producing industrial strapping that requires very high tensile strength and toughness.
  • Industrial Fiber Grade: Suitable for producing geotextiles, industrial ropes, and machine-made carpet backing.

Key Advantages

  • Exceptional Tensile Strength: High stretchability without breaking during the yarn production process.
  • Dimensional Stability: Maintenance of precise dimensions during tape production, resulting in uniform fabric output.
  • Excellent Processability: High output rates in textile extruders with minimal waste.
  • Environmental Resistance: Optimal stability against moisture and temperature changes for long-term product storage.

Primary Applications

  • Packaging Industry: Production of woven bags and Flexible Intermediate Bulk Containers (FIBC/Jumbo bags).
  • Civil & Agriculture: Production of geotextile fabrics for road sub-base reinforcement and weed control covers.
  • Logistics: Production of pallet strapping and export packaging bands.
  • Textile Industry: Production of machine-made carpet backing and artificial grass.

Packaging & Supply

Maintaining granule integrity for optimal extruder performance:

  • 25 kg Bags: Standard packaging for ease of handling.
  • Jumbo Bags (1 Ton): Economical packaging for high-volume operations.

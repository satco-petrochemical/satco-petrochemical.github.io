Ammonium Sulfate: Efficient Nitrogen Fertilizer

HS Code: 310221

Ammonium Sulfate is a widely used agricultural fertilizer that plays a key role in improving crop quality and yield by providing both nitrogen and sulfur simultaneously. It is particularly effective for alkaline and calcareous soils that require a reduction in pH. SATCO supplies high-grade Ammonium Sulfate tailored to meet the rigorous demands of agricultural and industrial sectors.

Product Grades & Variations

SATCO ensures a reliable supply of Ammonium Sulfate in grades optimized for specific requirements:

  • Agricultural Grade (Granular): The industry standard for field application, with excellent spreadability for mechanical fertilizer applicators and superior soil solubility.
  • Crystalline Grade: High-purity product, suitable for precision agricultural applications and as a raw material in various industrial processes.

Key Advantages

  • Dual Nutrition: Provides simultaneous nitrogen for vegetative growth and sulfur for the production of proteins and amino acids in plants.
  • Soil Amendment: Assists in lowering the pH of alkaline soils, increasing plant access to essential micronutrients.
  • Consistent Quality: Produced with strict impurity control to ensure uniform performance in the field.
  • Supply Chain Safety: Rigorous adherence to international standards for the storage and transport of bulk agricultural commodities.

Primary Applications

  • Arable Farming: An ideal fertilizer for crops such as rice, wheat, corn, and cotton to increase yield and crop quality.
  • Horticulture: Suitable for fruit trees and plants that thrive in slightly acidic soil environments.
  • Chemical Manufacturing: Used as a raw material in the production of various chemicals and pharmaceuticals.
  • Food Industry: Utilized as an acidity regulator in specific food processing operations.

Formic Acid: A Versatile Organic Acid in Chemical and Agricultural Industries

HS Code: 291511

Formic Acid, the simplest member of the carboxylic acid family, is a vital chemical with widespread applications across various industries. At SATCO, this product is supplied with the highest standards of purity and stability for industrial, agricultural, and livestock applications.

Grades and Product Variety

  • Industrial Grade: Featuring high purity, suitable for use in leather tanning, textile processing, and as an intermediate in chemical synthesis.
  • Agri/Feed Grade: Featuring minimal impurities, intended for use in the production of animal feed additives (as a preservative) and pest control.

Key Advantages

  • High Acidity: Strong acidic properties combined with high biodegradability.
  • Chemical Stability: Consistent quality maintained throughout storage and transport conditions.
  • Multipurpose Performance: Excellent efficiency as a coagulant, buffering agent, and preservative across diverse industries.
  • Safety Standards: Strict adherence to specialized packaging protocols for the safe management of this corrosive substance to prevent leakage.

Primary Applications

  • Leather and Textile Industry: Used as a tanning agent, skin soaking agent, and pH regulator in dyeing processes.
  • Agriculture and Livestock: Used as a preservative in silage to prevent mold and maintain nutritional value.
  • Chemical Synthesis: A raw material in the production of pharmaceuticals, pesticides, and industrial intermediates.
  • Rubber Industry: Used as a coagulant in the production of natural latex.

Packaging and Supply

  • 25L Jerricans / 250 kg Drums: Designed for workshop use and safe distribution.
  • IBC Tank (1000 Liters): Designed for bulk supply and large-scale industrial production lines.

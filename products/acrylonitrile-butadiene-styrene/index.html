Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene (ABS): High Impact Resistance and Structural Strength in Plastic Engineering

HS Code: 390330

Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene (ABS) is a widely used engineering polymer known for its excellent balance of impact resistance, hardness, and ease of processability across various industries. At SATCO, ABS grades are supplied with guaranteed quality to meet the technical requirements of manufacturing industries.

Product Grades & Variations

  • General Purpose Grade: Ideal for structural parts and plastic housings with desirable surface appearance.
  • Heat Resistant Grade: Designed for applications requiring dimensional stability at elevated temperatures.
  • Flame Retardant Grade: Suitable for electronic equipment and vehicle interior parts with strict safety requirements.

Key Advantages

  • High Impact Resistance: Excellent toughness and resistance to physical impacts.
  • Hardness and Strength: Proper mechanical stability for high-pressure applications.
  • Excellent Processability: Easy injection molding and high-quality surface finish.
  • Paintability and Printability: An ideal surface for painting and post-production services.

Primary Applications

  • Electronics: Household appliance housings, computer cases, telephones, and office equipment.
  • Automotive: Interior automotive trim, dashboards, and ventilation system components.
  • Sports Equipment and Toys: Production of durable parts with high safety standards.
  • Industrial Uses: Protective housings, gears, and pipe fittings.

Packaging & Supply

Standardized packaging and transport:

  • 25 kg Bags: Standardized for controlled transport and precise usage.
  • Jumbo Bags (1 Ton): Optimized for high-volume industries and mass production lines.

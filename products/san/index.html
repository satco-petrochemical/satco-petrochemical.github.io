Styrene Acrylonitrile (SAN): Optical Clarity and Chemical Resistance in Modern Engineering

HS Code: 390320

Styrene Acrylonitrile (SAN) is a high-performance engineering thermoplastic copolymer known for its superior balance of optical clarity, mechanical strength, and excellent chemical resistance against oils and detergents. At SATCO, SAN grades are supplied with guaranteed quality to meet the requirements of sensitive and high-precision industries.

Grades and Product Variety

  • General Purpose Grade: Ideal for producing rigid, transparent parts with high surface gloss.
  • High-Flow Grade: Designed for molding complex geometries and thin-walled components.
  • UV-Stabilized Grade: Engineered for outdoor applications requiring long-term stability against solar radiation.

Key Advantages

  • Superior Clarity: Exceptional optical transparency and high gloss similar to glass.
  • Outstanding Chemical Resistance: Excellent stability when exposed to food substances, oils, and cleaning agents.
  • Dimensional Stability: Maintains precise shapes and dimensions under mechanical stress.
  • Processability: Excellent flow characteristics for high-quality surface finishes in injection molding.

Primary Applications

  • Home Appliances: Mixing bowls, pitchers, refrigerator interior parts, and dishwasher components.
  • Cosmetic Packaging: Premium containers for creams, powders, and luxurious retail packaging.
  • Stationery: High-quality transparent pens, rulers, and office equipment.
  • Automotive & Medical: Interior automotive panels, medical housing, and laboratory equipment.

Packaging and Supply

  • 25 kg Moisture-proof Bags: Standardized for controlled transport and precise usage.
  • 1-Ton Jumbo Bags: Optimized for high-volume industrial production and mass export.

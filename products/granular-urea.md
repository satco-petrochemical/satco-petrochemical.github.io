Produced through the synthesis of ammonia and carbon dioxide, our Granular Urea is engineered for optimal release rates and minimal dusting during handling[cite: 1]. Its spherical, uniform granule structure ensures consistent application, making it the preferred choice for both large-scale agricultural operations and complex industrial applications[cite: 1].

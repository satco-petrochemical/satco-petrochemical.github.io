  <h1 style="color: #d4af37; text-align: center; margin-bottom: 20px; text-transform: uppercase;">Anhydrous Ammonia</h1>
  <p style="text-align: center; color: #d4af37; font-size: 1.1em; margin-bottom: 30px;">HS Code: 281410</p>

  <p style="line-height: 1.8; font-size: 1.1em; margin-bottom: 20px; text-align: justify;">
    Anhydrous Ammonia is a critical chemical compound composed of nitrogen and hydrogen, serving as a fundamental pillar of the global chemical industry. Maintained as a pressurized liquid, it represents the most concentrated source of nitrogen available, playing a key role in the synthesis of fertilizers, industrial explosives, and pharmaceutical intermediates.
  </p>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Product Grades & Variations</h2>
  <p style="line-height: 1.8; font-size: 1.05em; margin-bottom: 20px; text-align: justify;">
    SATCO supplies high-purity Anhydrous Ammonia sourced from leading production facilities to cater to diverse industrial requirements:
  </p>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>Agricultural Grade:</strong> High-purity ammonia designed for direct soil injection, ensuring optimal nitrogen delivery for agricultural crop development.</li>
    <li><strong>Industrial/Refrigeration Grade:</strong> Processed with strictly controlled impurity levels—specifically ultra-low moisture and oil content—optimized for industrial cooling systems and chemical synthesis.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Key Advantages</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>Exceptional Nitrogen Density:</strong> Contains over 82% nitrogen, offering the highest efficiency among all nitrogenous fertilizers.</li>
    <li><strong>High Purity:</strong> Produced through precision processes to minimize moisture and metallic impurities.</li>
    <li><strong>Versatility:</strong> A vital raw material for the synthesis of Nitric Acid, Urea, and various polymers.</li>
    <li><strong>Supply Reliability:</strong> Consistent quality and stable availability for large-scale international shipments.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Primary Applications</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>Fertilizer Production:</strong> A fundamental raw material for manufacturing Urea, Ammonium Nitrate, and other complex fertilizers.</li>
    <li><strong>Chemical Synthesis:</strong> Essential for the production of Nitric Acid, cyanides, and plastic precursors.</li>
    <li><strong>Industrial Refrigeration:</strong> Used as a highly efficient refrigerant (R-717) in large-scale cold storage and food processing facilities.</li>
    <li><strong>Environmental Control:</strong> Utilized in Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) processes to reduce NOx emissions in heavy industry.</li>
  </ul>

Anhydrous Ammonia: Industrial Feedstock and Fertilizer Base

HS Code: 281410

Anhydrous Ammonia is a critical chemical compound composed of nitrogen and hydrogen, serving as a fundamental pillar of the global chemical industry. Maintained as a pressurized liquid, it represents the most concentrated source of nitrogen available, playing a key role in the synthesis of fertilizers, industrial explosives, and pharmaceutical intermediates.

Product Grades & Variations

SATCO supplies high-purity Anhydrous Ammonia sourced from leading production facilities to cater to diverse industrial requirements:

  • Agricultural Grade: High-purity ammonia designed for direct soil injection, ensuring optimal nitrogen delivery for agricultural crop development.
  • Industrial/Refrigeration Grade: Processed with strictly controlled impurity levels—specifically ultra-low moisture and oil content—optimized for industrial cooling systems and chemical synthesis.

Key Advantages

  • Exceptional Nitrogen Density: Contains over 82% nitrogen, offering the highest efficiency among all nitrogenous fertilizers.
  • High Purity: Produced through precision processes to minimize moisture and metallic impurities.
  • Versatility: A vital raw material for the synthesis of Nitric Acid, Urea, and various polymers.
  • Supply Reliability: Consistent quality and stable availability for large-scale international shipments.

Primary Applications

  • Fertilizer Production: A fundamental raw material for manufacturing Urea, Ammonium Nitrate, and other complex fertilizers.
  • Chemical Synthesis: Essential for the production of Nitric Acid, cyanides, and plastic precursors.
  • Industrial Refrigeration: Used as a highly efficient refrigerant (R-717) in large-scale cold storage and food processing facilities.
  • Environmental Control: Utilized in Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) processes to reduce NOx emissions in heavy industry.

Packaging & Supply

Considering the chemical nature of Ammonia, SATCO adheres to rigorous safety standards regarding handling and supply:

  • ISO Tanks: The preferred solution for secure, standard international transport.
  • LPG Carriers: Dedicated gas vessels utilized for high-tonnage orders and global maritime trade.

Polypropylene Copolymer (PP Copolymer): Optimal Balance Between Flexibility and Impact Resistance

HS Code: 390230

Polypropylene Copolymer (PP Copolymer), due to its modified molecular structure, offers significantly better impact resistance compared to homopolymers, especially at low temperatures[cite: 6]. At SATCO, this product is supplied with structural quality guaranteed for a wide range of industrial applications[cite: 6].

Grades and Product Variety

  • Impact Copolymer Grade: Features very high impact resistance, suitable for manufacturing hard and durable parts[cite: 6].
  • Random Copolymer Grade: Features high clarity and excellent optical properties, suitable for decorative and packaging applications[cite: 6].

Key Advantages

  • Excellent Impact Resistance: Superior performance against impact even in cold environments[cite: 6].
  • Good Flexibility: Strong resistance against stress cracking[cite: 6].
  • High Processability: Excellent capability in injection molding and extrusion[cite: 6].
  • Lightweight: Reduced transportation costs and ease of final use[cite: 6].

Primary Applications

  • Packaging Industry: Production of food storage containers, bottles, and flexible housings[cite: 6].
  • Household Appliances: Interior parts for washing machines and dishwashers[cite: 6].
  • Automotive: Production of bumpers, dashboards, and interior trim parts[cite: 6].
  • Industrial Equipment: Pipe fittings, battery cases, and bend-resistant parts[cite: 6].

Packaging and Supply

  • 25 kg Bags: Standardized for controlled transport and precise usage[cite: 6].
  • 1-Ton Jumbo Bags: Optimized for high-volume industries and mass production lines[cite: 6].

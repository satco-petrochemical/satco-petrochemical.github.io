Diammonium Phosphate (DAP): The Premier Fertilizer

HS Code: 310530

Diammonium Phosphate (DAP) is globally recognized as the most popular phosphorus-based fertilizer due to its high nutrient content and excellent physical handling characteristics. As a concentrated source of phosphorus and nitrogen, it is essential for stimulating robust root development and overall crop vitality. SATCO supplies high-grade DAP tailored to meet the rigorous demands of modern agricultural sectors and large-scale farming operations.

Product Grades & Variations

SATCO ensures a reliable supply of DAP in grades optimized for specific agricultural requirements:

  • Standard Agricultural Grade: The industry standard for wide-scale soil fertilization, formulated for optimal solubility and nutrient uptake efficiency.
  • High-Purity Granular Grade: Engineered with uniform particle size and superior moisture resistance, specifically designed for modern mechanized spreading equipment and precision farming.

Key Advantages

  • High Nutrient Concentration: Delivers an optimal 18-46-0 ratio, providing the primary building blocks for healthy plant growth.
  • Consistent Quality: Sourced to maintain uniform granule integrity, preventing dust formation and ensuring even field application.
  • Versatility: A critical fertilizer for a wide array of crops, including cereals, legumes, and industrial plantations.
  • Supply Chain Safety: Rigorous adherence to international standards for the storage and transport of bulk agricultural commodities.

Primary Applications

  • Cereal Production: A fundamental fertilizer for wheat, barley, and corn to accelerate early-season growth and development.
  • Soil Amendment: Actively used as a foundational base-dressing to replenish soil phosphorus levels for long-term productivity.
  • Fertigation Systems: Essential for specialized liquid-feed systems, providing immediate nutrient availability to root zones.
  • Industrial Manufacturing: A key component in the production of specialized fire retardants, fermentation nutrients, and water treatment chemicals.

Packaging & Supply

Safety and product integrity are paramount in the handling of DAP. SATCO utilizes robust logistical solutions:

  • Jumbo Bags (1 Ton / 1.5 Tons): The industry-preferred solution for secure, global maritime transport, ensuring protection from moisture and contamination.
  • Bulk Shipping: Efficient solutions for large-scale, cost-effective delivery to major international agricultural hubs.

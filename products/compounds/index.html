Compounds: Engineered Solutions for Specific Industrial Requirements

HS Code: 390190

Compounds are engineered mixtures obtained by blending base resins with specialized additives, fillers, and masterbatches to provide unique physical, mechanical, and thermal properties[cite: 5]. At SATCO, custom-tailored compounds are produced to optimize performance in complex applications[cite: 5].

Grades and Product Variety

  • Glass Fiber Reinforced Grade: For increased tensile strength and dimensional stability[cite: 5].
  • Flame Retardant Grade: For use in safety-sensitive industries and electronics[cite: 5].
  • Anti-Wear/Low Friction Grade: For moving and mechanical parts[cite: 5].

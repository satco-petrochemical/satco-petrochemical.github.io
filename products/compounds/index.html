Compounds: Engineered Solutions for Specific Industrial Requirements

HS Code: 390190

Compounds are engineered mixtures obtained by blending base resins with specialized additives, fillers, and masterbatches to provide unique physical, mechanical, and thermal properties. At SATCO, custom-tailored compounds are produced to optimize performance in complex applications.

Grades and Product Variety

  • Glass Fiber Reinforced Grade: For increased tensile strength and dimensional stability.
  • Flame Retardant Grade: For use in safety-sensitive industries and electronics.
  • Anti-Wear/Low Friction Grade: For moving and mechanical parts.

Key Advantages

  • Property Customization: Precise adjustment of characteristics such as hardness, toughness, and heat resistance.
  • Cost Optimization: Replacing expensive engineering materials with optimized compounds.
  • Consistency: Uniform properties throughout the entire production batch.
  • Increased Durability: Enhanced resistance to environmental and chemical factors.

Primary Applications

  • Automotive: Structural body parts, engine housings, and fuel system components.
  • Electronics: Computer equipment housings, switches, and connectors.
  • Household Appliances: Parts subjected to high thermal and mechanical stress.
  • Construction Industry: Technical parts and prefabricated connections with long service life.

Packaging and Supply

Standardized packaging for safe transport:

  • 25 kg Bags: Standardized for controlled transport and precise usage.
  • 1-Ton Jumbo Bags: Optimized for high-volume industries and mass production lines.

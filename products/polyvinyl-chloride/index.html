Polyvinyl Chloride (PVC): High Versatility in Construction and Infrastructure Industries

HS Code: 390410

Polyvinyl Chloride (PVC) is one of the most widely used polymers in the industry. Due to its ability to be compounded with various additives (such as plasticizers and stabilizers), it can cover a spectrum of products, ranging from rigid and hard to soft and flexible. At SATCO, we supply high-quality PVC resin grades to meet the diverse needs of the extrusion and molding industries.

Product Grades & Variations

Diversity in PVC grades enables the optimization of the final product:

  • Rigid PVC Grade: Ideal for producing sewage pipes, door and window profiles, construction sheets, and fittings.
  • Flexible/Soft PVC Grade: Suitable for producing hoses, electrical cable insulation, vinyl sheets, and medical applications.
  • Granule Grade (Compound): Ready-to-use formulations for injection and extrusion processes with high dimensional accuracy.

Key Advantages

  • Excellent Chemical Resistance: Unmatched stability against acids, bases, and salts, making it ideal for piping systems.
  • Durability and Longevity: Resistance to environmental erosion and corrosion, which minimizes maintenance costs.
  • Safety and Standards: Inherently self-extinguishing properties that enhance safety in building applications.
  • Processability: Ability to produce complex and precise shapes through various molding methods.

Primary Applications

  • Construction Industry: Water and sewage piping, construction profiles, and flooring.
  • Electrical & Electronics: Cable insulation, internal wiring, and cable ducts.
  • Medical Industry: Production of blood bags, IV tubing, and disposable sanitary equipment.
  • Packaging: Production of protective films and specific packaging containers.

Packaging & Supply

Maintaining chemical properties and resin purity throughout the supply chain:

  • 25 kg Bags: Standard packaging for protection against moisture and impurities.
  • Jumbo Bags (1 Ton): Suitable for facilitating transportation and use in high-capacity production lines.

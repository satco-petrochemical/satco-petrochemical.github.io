Key Advantages

  • High Chemical Purity: Ensures uniform reactivity and high efficiency in industrial formulations.
  • Optimized Particle Options: Available in both dense and light grades tailored to specific production requirements.
  • Effective Buffering Agent: Provides reliable pH control and stabilization in chemical processes.

Primary Applications

  • Glass Manufacturing: Acts as a primary fluxing agent in melting silica for container and flat glass production.
  • Detergent Industry: Used as a builder for water softening and maintaining alkalinity.
  • Chemical Processes: Essential raw material for the production of various sodium compounds.

Packaging and Supply

  • PP Bags (50 kg): Moisture-resistant packaging for standard logistics and handling.
  • Jumbo Bags: Suitable for bulk delivery in industrial-scale and large manufacturing facilities.

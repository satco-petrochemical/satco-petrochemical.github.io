Potash Fertilizers: Enhancing Crop Quality and Resilience

HS Code: 310420

Potash fertilizers, specifically Potassium Chloride (MOP) and Potassium Sulfate (SOP), are the primary sources of potassium for plants[cite: 6]. Potassium is known as the "quality element," playing a vital role in enzyme activation, osmotic pressure regulation, and enhancing plant resistance to environmental stresses such as drought and frost[cite: 6]. SATCO supplies a range of potash fertilizers with the highest purity standards to significantly improve agricultural product quality[cite: 6].

Product Grades & Variations

SATCO ensures the supply of potash fertilizers in various grades optimized for specific applications[cite: 6]:

  • Potassium Chloride (MOP): The most widely used and cost-effective potassium source, ideal for most field crops[cite: 6].
  • Potassium Sulfate (SOP): A superior option for chlorine-sensitive crops (such as tobacco, potatoes, and fruit trees) and saline soils[cite: 6].

Key Advantages

  • Product Quality Enhancement: Improves color, taste, size, and shelf life of fruits and vegetables[cite: 6].
  • Stress Resistance: Strengthens the plant's immune system against diseases and environmental stresses[cite: 6].
  • High Efficiency: Formulated for optimal solubility and rapid potassium availability for the plant[cite: 6].
  • Supply Chain Safety: Rigorous adherence to global standards for the storage and transport of bulk agricultural commodities[cite: 6].

Primary Applications

  • Horticulture: Essential for fruit trees, vineyards, and greenhouse crops to achieve export standards[cite: 6].
  • Cereal Farming: Strengthens stalks and increases grain weight for higher harvest yields[cite: 6].
  • Root Crops: Increases quality and volume of root and tuber crops (such as potatoes and sugar beets)[cite: 6].
  • Precision Agriculture: High mixability in fertigation systems and compound NPK fertilizers[cite: 6].

Packaging & Supply

Protecting the product against moisture and clumping is a priority[cite: 6]:

  • Jumbo Bags (1 Ton / 1.5 Tons): SATCO’s industry standard for secure and efficient international trade handling[cite: 6].
  • Bulk Shipping: Optimized for rapid port loading/unloading to reduce logistical costs[cite: 6].

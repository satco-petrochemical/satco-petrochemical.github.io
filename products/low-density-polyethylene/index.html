Low-Density Polyethylene (LDPE): Flexibility and Clarity in Packaging

HS Code: 390110

Low-Density Polyethylene (LDPE), due to its branched-chain structure, is a highly flexible, transparent, and moisture-resistant material. It is recognized as one of the most popular raw materials in the packaging industry and for producing thin films. SATCO supplies high-quality LDPE grades with guaranteed performance for a wide range of plastic products.

Product Grades & Variations

The diversity of LDPE grades allows for meeting various production requirements:

  • Film Grade: Suitable for producing plastic bags, food packaging films, shopping bags, and agricultural films (mulch).
  • Injection Molding Grade: Ideal for producing flexible parts, soft containers, toys, and industrial components that require high formability.
  • Coating Grade: Designed for coating paper, cardboard, and metallic foils to create a protective and impermeable layer.

Key Advantages

  • Excellent Flexibility: Extremely high formability, even at low temperatures.
  • Optimal Clarity: A superior option for packaging that requires displaying the product inside.
  • Chemical Resistance: Suitable stability against common solvents and chemicals.
  • Impermeability: High resistance to moisture and water vapor, making it an excellent protector for food products.

Primary Applications

  • Packaging Industry: Production of shrink films, stretch films, and flexible packaging bags.
  • Agriculture: Production of greenhouse nylon and cover films for soil preservation.
  • Medical Equipment: Manufacturing of flexible containers and disposable sanitary supplies.
  • Home Appliance Industry: Production of lid containers, squeeze bottles, and flexible parts for electrical appliances.

Packaging & Supply

Maintaining polymer properties during transportation:

  • 25 kg Bags: Common standard for protection against dust and contaminants.
  • Jumbo Bags (1 Ton): Optimized for easy handling in large-scale production operations.

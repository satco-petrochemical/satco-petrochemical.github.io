Sulfuric Acid: The Industrial Workhorse

HS Code: 280700

Sulfuric Acid (H2SO4) is universally recognized as the "King of Chemicals" due to its foundational role in global industrial processes. As a powerful mineral acid, it is essential for an extensive range of applications, from phosphate fertilizer production and petroleum refining to metal processing and chemical synthesis. SATCO supplies high-grade Sulfuric Acid tailored to meet the rigorous demands of modern industrial sectors.

Product Grades & Variations

SATCO ensures a reliable supply of Sulfuric Acid in grades optimized for specific industrial requirements:

  • Industrial Grade (98%): The industry standard for high-concentration applications, ideal for fertilizer production, water treatment, and heavy metal processing.
  • Battery Grade: Refined with ultra-low metallic impurity levels—particularly iron and heavy metals—specifically engineered for the maintenance and manufacturing of high-performance lead-acid batteries.

Key Advantages

  • High Reactivity: Optimal concentration for maximizing the efficiency of complex chemical reactions.
  • Consistent Quality: Sourced to maintain stringent impurity limits, protecting client infrastructure and preventing corrosion.
  • Versatility: A critical feedstock for a wide array of chemical and industrial manufacturing sectors.
  • Supply Chain Safety: Rigorous adherence to international safety standards for the handling and transport of corrosive materials.

Primary Applications

  • Fertilizer Industry: A fundamental reagent for the production of Phosphoric Acid and Superphosphate fertilizers.
  • Metallurgy: Essential for metal pickling, surface treatment, and ore extraction processes.
  • Petroleum Refining: Actively used as a catalyst in alkylation processes and general product purification.
  • Chemical Manufacturing: A key component in the production of detergents, synthetic dyes, pharmaceuticals, and industrial pigments.

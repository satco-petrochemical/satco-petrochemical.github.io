  <p style="line-height: 1.8; font-size: 1.1em; margin-bottom: 20px; text-align: justify;">

SATCO supplies high-purity Industrial and Technical Grade Sulfuric Acid, produced to meet stringent international quality standards. Known for its strong acid characteristics and dehydrating properties, it is indispensable for fertilizer production, metal processing, chemical synthesis, and water treatment applications.

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Primary Applications</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>Fertilizer Manufacturing:</strong> Crucial for the production of phosphoric acid, superphosphate fertilizers, and ammonium sulfate.</li>
    <li><strong>Mineral Processing & Metallurgy:</strong> Extensively used in leaching copper, uranium, and vanadium ores, as well as for metal pickling and cleaning.</li>
    <li><strong>Chemical Synthesis:</strong> Essential feedstock for producing synthetic detergents, dyes, pigments, and other industrial acids.</li>
    <li><strong>Water Treatment:</strong> Utilized for pH adjustment, neutralization of alkaline wastewater, and municipal water purification.</li>
  </ul>

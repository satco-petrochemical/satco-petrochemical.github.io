P-Xylene: Key Raw Material in the Polyester Value Chain

HS Code: 290243

P-Xylene is one of the most important aromatic hydrocarbons and a primary isomer of xylene, supplied as a clear, colorless liquid. It is recognized as a vital building block in the textile and packaging industries. At SATCO, p-xylene is supplied with the highest purity standards to meet the needs of the polyester industries.

Grades and Product Variety

  • Petrochemical/Polymer Grade: Extremely high purity, suitable for the synthesis of industrial polymers.

Key Advantages

  • Ultra-High Purity: Minimum impurities to ensure polymerization quality.
  • Symmetrical Structure: High efficiency in chemical conversion reactions to terephthalic acid.
  • Global Standard: Complete compliance with the technical specifications required by large manufacturing units.

Primary Applications

  • Purified Terephthalic Acid (PTA) Production: Primary application as the exclusive raw material for producing PTA and DMT.
  • Polyester and Textile Industries: Production of polyester fibers for apparel and industrial fabrics.
  • Packaging Industry: Manufacturing of plastic bottles (PET) and food packaging films.

Packaging and Supply

  • ISO Tank and Bulk: Safe and specialized for transporting industrial bulk volumes.
  • Specialized Metal Drums (200L): Standard and protected against environmental changes.

Sodium Sulfate: A Key Component in Detergent and Glass Industries

HS Code: 283311

Sodium Sulfate (Glauber's salt) is a versatile chemical and mineral compound that plays a vital role in a wide range of industrial processes due to its unique properties. At SATCO, this product is supplied with the highest quality standards to meet the diverse needs of various industries.

Grades and Product Variety

  • Detergent Grade: Featuring high purity and brilliant white color, used as a filler in detergent powder production to optimize performance and reduce manufacturing costs.
  • Industrial/Glass Grade: Featuring minimal impurities, suitable for improving melting efficiency and clarity in glass and ceramic manufacturing industries.

Key Advantages

  • Purity and Uniformity: Stable and consistent quality across all production batches.
  • Excellent Solubility: High solubility in water, making it an ideal choice for various chemical processes.
  • Chemical Stability: High resistance under various storage and processing conditions.
  • Safety Standards: Compliance with strict quality control principles in packaging and transport to prevent moisture absorption.

Primary Applications

  • Detergent Industry: A key ingredient in laundry powder production to regulate concentration and improve cleaning power.
  • Glass Industry: Used to eliminate air bubbles in molten glass and enhance the final product quality.
  • Textile Industry: Used as a leveling agent in the fabric dyeing process.
  • Paper and Pulp Industry: Assists in the paper pulp manufacturing process (Kraft process) and increases paper strength.

Packaging and Supply

  • 25 kg Bags (Laminated): Designed for maximum protection against moisture and ease of transport.
  • Big Bags (1 Ton): Suitable for industrial consumption and large-scale production line requirements.

Thermoplastic Elastomers (TPE): Combining Rubber Properties with Plastic Processing Ease

HS Code: 390190

Thermoplastic Elastomers (TPE) are a class of materials that combine the elastic and resilient properties of traditional rubbers with the processability of thermoplastics. At SATCO, these products are supplied to provide high performance and efficiency in production lines.

Grades and Product Variety

  • General Molding Grade: Suitable for manufacturing flexible parts with desirable dimensional stability.
  • Extrusion Grade: Designed for the production of profiles, hoses, and sealing strips.
  • High Hardness Grade: Ideal for engineering applications requiring a combination of rigidity and flexibility.

Key Advantages

  • Elasticity: Excellent rebound capability after applying pressure and deformation.
  • Thermal Processability: Capability for remelting and recycling, which reduces production waste.
  • Environmental Resistance: Suitable stability against weather conditions and temperature variations.
  • Excellent Adhesion: Ability to bond to various surfaces in dual-material parts (Overmolding).

Primary Applications

  • Automotive Industry: Door weatherstrips, dashboard components, gaskets, and vibration dampeners.
  • Consumer Goods: Tool handles, protective phone covers, and sports equipment.
  • Medical Equipment: Flexible hoses, caps, and sealing parts.
  • Electronics Industry: Flexible cables and protective coatings.

Packaging and Supply

  • 25 kg Bags: Standardized for controlled transport and precise usage.
  • 1-Ton Jumbo Bags: Optimized for high-volume industries and mass production lines.

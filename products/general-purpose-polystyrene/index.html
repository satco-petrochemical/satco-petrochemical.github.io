General Purpose Polystyrene (GPPS): Optical Clarity and Rigidity in Packaging and Injection moulding

HS Code: 390311

General Purpose Polystyrene (GPPS), also known as Crystal Polystyrene, is a hard, transparent, and rigid polymer[cite: 2]. Due to its exceptional optical properties and ease of moldability, it is widely used in various industries[cite: 2]. At SATCO, this grade is supplied with a controlled molecular weight to achieve the optimal melt flow required for manufacturing processes[cite: 2].

Product Grades & Variations

The diversity of GPPS grades enables the production of a wide range of items[cite: 2]:

  • Packaging Grade: Suitable for producing transparent disposable containers and food packaging[cite: 2].
  • Home Appliance Grade: Ideal for producing refrigerator interior components (such as clear shelves) and electronic housings[cite: 2].
  • Industrial Grade: For producing precision injection-molded parts, stationery, and toys[cite: 2].

Key Advantages

  • High Optical Clarity: Excellent light transmission, giving the final product a glass-like, brilliant appearance[cite: 2].
  • High Rigidity: Appropriate structural strength for parts that require shape retention under stress[cite: 2].
  • Excellent Processability: Optimal melt flow rate for injection into complex molds with minimal waste[cite: 2].
  • Dimensional Stability: Low shrinkage rate, ensuring high precision in part manufacturing[cite: 2].

Primary Applications

  • Containers & Packaging: Disposable tableware, cups, and protective packaging for cosmetics[cite: 2].
  • Home Appliances: Transparent refrigerator parts, audio and video equipment housings[cite: 2].
  • Education & Recreation: Pens, rulers, toy parts, and plastic models[cite: 2].
  • Optical Industries: Low-cost lenses, retail displays, and lighting fixtures[cite: 2].

Packaging & Supply

Maintaining granule quality to prevent contamination and moisture absorption[cite: 2]:

  • 25 kg Bags: Standard for safe handling and optimized storage[cite: 2].
  • Jumbo Bags (1 Ton): Economical for high-volume production operations[cite: 2].

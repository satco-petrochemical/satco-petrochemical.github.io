Styrene Monomer: Key Raw Material in Plastics and Resins Production

HS Code: 290250

Styrene Monomer is a clear, organic liquid recognized as one of the most vital building blocks in the petrochemical industry for producing various polymers, rubbers, and resins. At SATCO, this product is supplied with the highest standards of purity and stability.

Grades and Product Variety

  • Polymerization Grade: Featuring exceptionally high purity, optimized for the synthesis of polystyrene and other engineering polymers.

Key Advantages

  • High Chemical Purity: Minimum impurities such as ethylbenzene and light polymers to guarantee reaction quality.
  • Thermal and Storage Stability: Contains optimal amounts of inhibitor (TBC) to prevent unwanted polymerization during transport.
  • Excellent Reactivity: Rapid homopolymerization and copolymerization capability with various monomers.
  • Low Viscosity: Desirable fluidity and easy processability in industrial equipment.

Primary Applications

  • Polystyrene (PS) Production: Manufacturing general-purpose polystyrene (GPPS) and high-impact polystyrene (HIPS) for containers, packaging, and household parts.
  • Expandable Polystyrene (EPS) Foam: Widespread application in building insulation and shock-absorbing packaging.
  • Synthetic Rubbers: Producing styrene-butadiene rubber (SBR) for the tire and rubber manufacturing industry.
  • Unsaturated Polyester Resins: Application in manufacturing composites, fiberglass, and industrial coatings.

Packaging and Supply

  • Metal Drums (200 Liters): Resistant and standard.
  • ISO Tank and Bulk: To supply bulk industrial and petrochemical volumes.

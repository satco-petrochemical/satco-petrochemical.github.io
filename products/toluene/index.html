Toluene: Key Solvent and Raw Material in Chemical Industries

HS Code: 290230

Toluene is an aromatic liquid hydrocarbon with the chemical formula C7H8, recognized as one of the most widely used solvents and intermediate materials in the petrochemical, coatings, and chemical manufacturing industries. At SATCO, toluene is supplied with high purity and in compliance with international standards.

Grades and Product Variety

  • Industrial/Petrochemical Grade: High purity, suitable for solvent applications and chemical syntheses.

Key Advantages

  • Excellent Solvency Power: Outstanding performance as a solvent for resins, paints, adhesives, and coatings.
  • Optimal Reactivity: Active methyl group on the aromatic ring for synthesizing various industrial derivatives.
  • Controlled Volatility: Balanced evaporation rate for coating and industrial applications.

Primary Applications

  • Chemical Production: Raw material for producing benzene, xylene, and benzoic acid.
  • Paint and Resin Industries: Used as a base solvent in the formulation of paints, lacquers, and adhesives.
  • Petrochemical Industry: Enhancing octane number in gasoline and utilization in extraction processes.
  • Pharmaceuticals and Cosmetics: Serving as an intermediate solvent in pharmaceutical syntheses.

Packaging and Supply

  • Specialized Metal Drums (200L): Standard and durable for the safe transport of aromatic liquids.
  • ISO Tank and Bulk: Suitable for large-scale industrial orders.

Monoethylene Glycol (MEG) & Diethylene Glycol (DEG): Essential Glycols for Industrial and Polymeric Applications

At SATCO, we supply high-purity Ethylene Glycols (MEG and DEG) designed to meet the rigorous quality standards of global polyester, antifreeze, resin, and chemical processing industries.

1. Monoethylene Glycol (MEG)

HS Code: 290531

Monoethylene Glycol is the simplest glycol and one of the most vital intermediate chemical compounds playing a crucial role in the petrochemical industry. At SATCO, MEG is supplied with high purity for polymeric and industrial applications.

Grades and Product Variety

  • Fiber/PET Grade: Featuring extremely high purity, specifically suitable for producing polyester fibers, petrochemical resins, and plastic bottles.
  • Industrial Grade: Suitable for heat transfer systems and industrial fluids.

Key Advantages

  • High Chemical Purity: Minimum water and impurity levels to prevent disruptions in the polymerization process.
  • Optimal Thermal Stability: Reliable performance in high-temperature industrial processes.
  • Excellent Miscibility: Complete solubility in water and polar solvents.
  • Suitable Boiling Point: Ideal for adjusting thermal properties of fluids.

Primary Applications

  • Polymer Production: The main raw material in manufacturing polyester fibers and polyethylene terephthalate (PET).
  • Antifreeze Industries: Formulation of coolants for internal combustion engines and HVAC systems.
  • Oil and Gas Industry: Dehydration agent for natural gas in refineries.
  • Chemical Production: Manufacturing alkyd resins and plasticizers.

Packaging and Supply

  • Metal Drums (220 Liters): For safe handling.
  • ISO Tank and Bulk: To supply bulk industrial volumes.

2. Diethylene Glycol (DEG)

HS Code: 290533

Diethylene Glycol is a high-boiling-point diol compound widely used as a solvent, plasticizer, and raw material in chemical synthesis. At SATCO, this product is provided with premium quality standards.

Grades and Product Variety

  • Industrial Grade: Optimized for general applications, resin manufacturing, and gas fluids.

Key Advantages

  • High Solvency Power: Exceptional ability to dissolve a wide range of resinous and coloring materials.
  • Low Volatility: Superior stability at high temperatures and reduced evaporation during processes.
  • Good Hygroscopicity: Effective moisture absorption in gas-drying processes.
  • Desired Reactivity: Suitable for producing unsaturated polyesters and plasticizers.

Primary Applications

  • Oil and Gas Industry: Dehydration and sweetening/drying of natural gas.
  • Resin and Plastic Production: Manufacturing unsaturated polyesters, urethanes, and plasticizers.
  • Cosmetics and Personal Care: Used as a humectant and solvent in specific products (subject to grade standards).
  • Lubricants and Inks: Manufacturing printing inks and hydraulic fluids.

Packaging and Supply

  • Metal Drums (220 Liters): Standard for warehousing and transport.
  • ISO Tank and Bulk: For mass production lines.

  <h1 style="color: #d4af37; text-align: center; margin-bottom: 20px; text-transform: uppercase;">Diammonium Phosphate (DAP)</h1>
  <p style="text-align: center; color: #d4af37; font-size: 1.1em; margin-bottom: 30px;">HS Code: 310530</p>

  <p style="line-height: 1.8; font-size: 1.1em; margin-bottom: 20px; text-align: justify;">
    Diammonium Phosphate (DAP) is the world’s most widely used phosphorus fertilizer, highly valued for its high nutrient content and excellent physical properties. SATCO supplies premium-grade DAP granules, manufactured through the precise reaction of phosphoric acid and ammonia, ensuring optimal solubility, rapid nutrient availability, and uniform distribution for diverse agricultural applications.
  </p>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Product Grades & Variations</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>Standard Agricultural Grade:</strong> Granulated uniform dark or clear brown/black spheres optimized for dry bulk blending and direct soil application.</li>
    <li><strong>Soluble Grade (Fertigation):</strong> High-purity micro-granules designed for irrigation and liquid feeding systems without clogging emitters.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Key Advantages</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>Dual Nutrient Power:</strong> Provides an excellent combination of nitrogen and phosphorus (18-46-0) to stimulate robust early root development.</li>
    <li><strong>High Solubility:</strong> Dissolves quickly in soil moisture to make phosphate immediately accessible to developing crops.</li>
    <li><strong>Physiologically Alkaline Effect:</strong> Initially dissolves to form an alkaline reaction around the granule, enhancing early root zone uptake in acidic soils.</li>
    <li><strong>Superior Granule Integrity:</strong> High crushing strength minimizes dust formation and handling losses during shipping.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Primary Applications</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>Broad-Acre Agriculture:</strong> Essential starter fertilizer for cereals, wheat, corn, cotton, and oilseeds.</li>
    <li><strong>Bulk Blending:</strong> Extensively used as a primary component in custom NPK fertilizer blends.</li>
    <li><strong>Fire Retardants:</strong> Utilized in specialized commercial fire-fighting formulations and wood treatment.</li>
  </ul>

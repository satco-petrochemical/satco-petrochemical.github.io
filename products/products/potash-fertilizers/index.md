  <h1 style="color: #d4af37; text-align: center; margin-bottom: 20px; text-transform: uppercase;">Potash Fertilizers</h1>
  <p style="text-align: center; color: #d4af37; font-size: 1.1em; margin-bottom: 30px;">HS Code: 310420</p>

  <p style="line-height: 1.8; font-size: 1.1em; margin-bottom: 20px; text-align: justify;">
    Potash Fertilizers are essential potassium-rich plant nutrients vital for regulating water uptake, enzyme activation, and overall crop quality. SATCO supplies premium-grade potassium fertilizers—including Muriate of Potash (MOP / Potassium Chloride) and Sulfate of Potash (SOP / Potassium Sulfate)—engineered to enhance stress tolerance, improve root strength, and maximize harvest yields across diverse agricultural soils.
  </p>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Product Grades & Variations</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>Muriate of Potash (MOP / KCl):</strong> The most concentrated and economical potassium source, ideal for field crops, cereals, and non-chloride-sensitive plants.</li>
    <li><strong>Sulfate of Potash (SOP / K2SO4):</strong> Premium chlorine-free potassium fertilizer combined with sulfur, specially formulated for chloride-sensitive crops like fruits, vegetables, and tobacco.</li>
    <li><strong>Soluble Fertigation Grade:</strong> High-purity ultra-fine crystals designed for modern drip irrigation and foliar application systems.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Key Advantages</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>High Potassium Content:</strong> Delivers superior K2O concentrations ensuring efficient plant metabolic functions and osmoregulation.</li>
    <li><strong>Improved Crop Quality:</strong> Enhances fruit size, color, sugar content, and overall shelf-life of agricultural produce.</li>
    <li><strong>Stress Resistance:</strong> Strengthens plant cell walls, increasing tolerance against drought, frost, lodging, and fungal diseases.</li>
    <li><strong>Excellent Physical Condition:</strong> Treated with anti-caking agents to maintain free-flowing properties during long-distance maritime shipping.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Primary Applications</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>Commercial Agriculture:</strong> Essential basal and top-dressing fertilizer for corn, wheat, rice, soybeans, and cotton.</li>
    <li><strong>Horticulture & Orchards:</strong> Widely used in vineyards, citrus groves, potato farming, and high-value vegetable production.</li>
    <li><strong>Industrial Uses:</strong> Utilized in potassium salt manufacturing, soap production, and chemical synthesis.</li>
  </ul>

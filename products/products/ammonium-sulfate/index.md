  <h1 style="color: #d4af37; text-align: center; margin-bottom: 20px; text-transform: uppercase;">Ammonium Sulfate</h1>
  <p style="text-align: center; color: #d4af37; font-size: 1.1em; margin-bottom: 30px;">HS Code: 310221</p>

  <p style="line-height: 1.8; font-size: 1.1em; margin-bottom: 20px; text-align: justify;">
    Ammonium Sulfate is a premier inorganic salt providing an ideal combination of nitrogen and sulfur, two essential macronutrients required for high-yield crop production. SATCO supplies high-purity crystalline and granular Ammonium Sulfate, widely recognized for its excellent storage properties, low hygroscopicity, and exceptional performance in alkaline and calcareous soils.
  </p>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Product Grades & Variations</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>Caprolactam Grade / Crystalline:</strong> Fine crystalline powder characterized by rapid solubility, perfect for liquid fertilizer mixing and specialized fertigation.</li>
    <li><strong>Granular Grade:</strong> Compacted uniform granules designed specifically for dry bulk blending with other fertilizers and direct mechanical spreading.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Key Advantages</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>Dual Nutrients (N-S):</strong> Contains 21% nitrogen in the ammonium form and 24% sulfur in sulfate form, promoting optimal protein synthesis in crops.</li>
    <li><strong>Acidifying Action:</strong> Effectively lowers pH in alkaline or calcareous soils, increasing the availability of essential micronutrients like phosphorus and iron.</li>
    <li><strong>Low Moisture Absorption:</strong> Exhibits low hygroscopicity, meaning it resists caking and remains free-flowing even in humid environments.</li>
    <li><strong>Versatile Application:</strong> Highly compatible for blending with urea, potassium chloride, and other granular fertilizers without chemical degradation.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Primary Applications</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>Agricultural Fertilization:</strong> Widely applied to sulfur-loving crops such as wheat, corn, rice, oilseeds, potatoes, and various fruits.</li>
    <li><strong>Industrial Manufacturing:</strong> Used as an auxiliary processing agent in textile manufacturing, leather tanning, and fire-retardant powder production.</li>
    <li><strong>Water Treatment:</strong> Utilized in municipal water treatment facilities alongside chlorine to form monochloramine for disinfection.</li>
  </ul>

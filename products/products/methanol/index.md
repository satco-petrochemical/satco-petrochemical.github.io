  <p style="line-height: 1.8; font-size: 1.1em; margin-bottom: 20px; text-align: justify;">
    Methanol, also known as methyl alcohol, is a vital chemical building block and clean-burning energy source. Produced primarily from synthesis gas, SATCO supplies high-purity Grade AA Methanol meeting international standards for chemical synthesis, formaldehyde production, and fuel blending applications.
  </p>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Product Grades & Variations</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>Grade AA (Pure Methanol):</strong> High-purity chemical feedstock designed for industrial synthesis, featuring ultra-low water and acetone content.</li>
    <li><strong>Fuel Grade:</strong> Optimized for blending into gasoline and use in direct methanol fuel cells or industrial boilers.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Key Advantages</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>High Purity:</strong> Minimum 99.85% purity ensuring optimal performance in chemical reactions.</li>
    <li><strong>Low Impurities:</strong> Strict control over ethanol, water, and permanganate time.</li>
    <li><strong>Versatility:</strong> Serves as a precursor for a vast range of downstream petrochemicals.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Primary Applications</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>Formaldehyde Production:</strong> Used to manufacture adhesives, resins, and plastics.</li>
    <li><strong>Acetic Acid:</strong> A major derivative utilized in paints, coatings, and solvents.</li>
    <li><strong>Energy & Fuel:</strong> Utilized in MTBE production, biodiesel, and direct fuel applications.</li>
  </ul>

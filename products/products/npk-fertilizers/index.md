  <h1 style="color: #d4af37; text-align: center; margin-bottom: 20px; text-transform: uppercase;">NPK Fertilizers</h1>
  <p style="text-align: center; color: #d4af37; font-size: 1.1em; margin-bottom: 30px;">HS Code: 310520</p>

  <p style="line-height: 1.8; font-size: 1.1em; margin-bottom: 20px; text-align: justify;">
    NPK Fertilizers are comprehensive, balanced compound fertilizers containing the three primary plant macronutrients: Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), and Potassium (K). SATCO supplies premium multi-nutrient NPK formulations tailored to meet the specific nutritional demands of various crops at different growth stages, ensuring maximum yield, improved root development, and enhanced resistance to environmental stress.
  </p>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Product Grades & Formulations</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>Balanced Formulations (e.g., 20-20-20):</strong> Universal all-purpose fertilizers suitable for general vegetative growth and maintenance across diverse crops.</li>
    <li><strong>High-Nitrogen Formulations (e.g., 30-10-10):</strong> Designed for early growth stages to stimulate leaf development and stem vigor.</li>
    <li><strong>High-Phosphorus/Potassium Formulations (e.g., 12-12-36 / 15-30-15):</strong> Specialized blends to support robust root establishment, flowering, and fruit setting/maturation.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Key Advantages</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>Complete Nutrition:</strong> Delivers all three vital macronutrients in every single uniform granule or water-soluble crystal.</li>
    <li><strong>High Solubility & Purity:</strong> Formulated from premium raw materials ensuring complete dissolution without clogging irrigation systems (fertigation).</li>
    <li><strong>Customizable Ratios:</strong> Flexible manufacturing ratios to match specific soil testing requirements and crop phenological stages.</li>
    <li><strong>Enhanced Nutrient Uptake:</strong> Contains synergistic microelements that improve overall nutrient assimilation efficiency.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Primary Applications</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>Greenhouse & Open-Field Horticulture:</strong> Applied via drip irrigation (fertigation) for vegetables, fruits, and flowers.</li>
    <li><strong>Broad-Acre Farming:</strong> Used as basal or side-dress application for cereals, industrial crops, and orchards.</li>
    <li><strong>Turf & Landscaping:</strong> Ideal for professional turf management, golf courses, and commercial nurseries.</li>
  </ul>

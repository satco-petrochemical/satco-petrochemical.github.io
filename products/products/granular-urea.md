  <p style="line-height: 1.8; font-size: 1.1em; margin-bottom: 20px; text-align: justify;">
    Granular Urea is a premium-grade solid nitrogen fertilizer widely utilized in global agriculture for its high nutrient content and excellent physical characteristics. SATCO supplies export-grade granular urea featuring high crushing strength, uniform particle size distribution, and resistance to caking during storage and maritime transport.
  </p>

  <h2 style="color: #d4af37; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 10px; margin-top: 40px;">Key Advantages</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li><strong>High Nitrogen Content:</strong> Contains 46% nitrogen, providing a concentrated nutrient source for rapid and healthy crop development.</li>
    <li><strong>Superior Physical Strength:</strong> Large, hard granules minimize dust formation and ensure smooth mechanical spreading in large-scale farming.</li>
    <li><strong>Excellent Storage Stability:</strong> Specially treated with conditioning agents to prevent moisture absorption and caking.</li>
  </ul>

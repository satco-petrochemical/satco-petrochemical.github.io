High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE): Strength and Stability in Packaging and Industry

HS Code: 390120

High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) is one of the most widely used polymers in global industries, known for its high strength-to-density ratio, excellent impact resistance, and outstanding chemical stability. SATCO supplies a variety of HDPE grades with guaranteed quality for the production of a wide range of industrial and consumer products.

Product Grades & Variations

The diversity of HDPE grades allows for meeting various production requirements:

  • Injection Molding Grade: Suitable for producing rigid parts, buckets, industrial crates, and packaging containers that require high structural strength.
  • Blow Molding Grade: Ideal for producing detergent bottles, chemical storage containers, and tanks that require Environmental Stress Cracking Resistance (ESCR).
  • Extrusion/Pipe Grade: Designed for the production of water and gas transmission pipes and cable conduits with a long service life.

Key Advantages

  • High Resistance: Stability against chemicals, acids, and bases, and resistance to harsh weather conditions.
  • Durability & Safety: Non-toxicity and recyclability, making it an environmentally friendly option.
  • Production Efficiency: Optimal flowability in production processes, leading to reduced waste and increased production line speed.
  • Versatility: The ability to produce parts that are lightweight yet extremely strong.

Primary Applications

  • Packaging Industry: Production of food, pharmaceutical, and chemical containers.
  • Civil Infrastructure: Production of water supply pipes, sewage systems, and agricultural irrigation systems.
  • Industrial Components: Manufacturing of automotive parts, home appliances, and safety equipment.
  • Household Products: Production of storage containers, industrial waste bins, and injection-molded goods.

Packaging & Supply

Maintaining raw material purity during transportation:

  • 25 kg Bags: Standard packaging to protect against contamination and moisture.
  • Jumbo Bags (1 Ton): Suitable for large-scale production to facilitate storage and loading.

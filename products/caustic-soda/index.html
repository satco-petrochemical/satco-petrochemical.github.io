Caustic Soda (Sodium Hydroxide)

HS Code: 281512 (Liquid) / 281511 (Solid)[cite: 7]

Caustic Soda, or Sodium Hydroxide (NaOH), is one of the strongest inorganic bases and essential industrial chemicals, supplied in both liquid and solid (flakes/pearls) forms[cite: 7]. At SATCO, this product is supplied with high purity and guaranteed quality standards for various industrial applications[cite: 7].

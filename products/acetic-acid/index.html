Acetic Acid: A Key Organic Acid in Chemical and Manufacturing Industries

HS Code: 291521

Acetic Acid, also known as ethanoic acid, is one of the most important carboxylic acids, widely used as a raw material and solvent in industry. At SATCO, this product is supplied with the highest quality standards for industrial and commercial applications.

Grades and Product Variety

  • Industrial Grade: Featuring high purity, suitable for the production of vinyl acetate monomer (VAM), acetic anhydride, and cellulose acetates.
  • Food/Pharma Grade: Featuring minimal impurities, suitable for sensitive processes and food packaging industries.

Key Advantages

  • Purity and Stability: Uniform and consistent quality across all production batches.
  • Solvency Power: Excellent ability to dissolve various organic and inorganic compounds.
  • Desired Reactivity: An ideal structural raw material for synthesizing a wide range of chemical derivatives.
  • Safety Standards: Compliance with strict quality control principles in packaging and transport.

Primary Applications

  • Polymer Production: Manufacturing vinyl acetate monomer (VAM) and polyvinyl acetate (PVA).
  • Chemical Industry: Production of various acetates, acetic anhydride, and industrial solvents.
  • Textile and Dye Industry: As a pH regulator and color fixer in dyeing processes.
  • Food and Pharmaceutical Industries: Used as an acidity regulator and raw material in the synthesis of various drugs.

Packaging and Supply

  • Polyethylene Drums (220 Liters): Standard for safe transport and storage.
  • ISO Tank and Bulk: To supply bulk orders and large-scale industrial production lines.

Vinyl Acetate Monomer (VAM): Strategic Raw Material in Polymer Industries

HS Code: 291532

Vinyl Acetate Monomer is a colorless, organic liquid acting as a key building block in the production of a wide range of industrial polymers, resins, and emulsions. At SATCO, VAM is supplied with the highest purity and safety standards for advanced industrial applications.

Grades and Product Variety

  • Polymerization Grade: Featuring exceptionally high purity, specifically suitable for producing polyvinyl acetate (PVA), polyvinyl alcohol (PVOH), and copolymers.

Key Advantages

  • High Purity: Minimum impurities such as acetaldehyde and acetic acid to ensure flawless polymerization.
  • Excellent Reactivity: High speed in chain reactions and the synthesis of various polymers.
  • Storage Stability: Contains appropriate inhibitors to prevent spontaneous polymerization during transport.
  • Optimal Compatibility: Ability to blend and copolymerize with various monomers such as ethylene and acrylates.

Primary Applications

  • Polyvinyl Acetate (PVA) Production: Manufacturing adhesives, wood glues, and industrial sealants.
  • Polyvinyl Alcohol (PVOH) Production: Manufacturing fibers, water-soluble packaging films, and paper coatings.
  • Ethylene Vinyl Acetate (EVA) Copolymers: Applications in foams, hot-melt adhesives, and industrial cables.
  • Paint and Resin Industries: Production of water-based emulsions and architectural paints.

Packaging and Supply

  • Specialized Metal Drums (200 Liters): Resistant and safe for volatile chemical compounds.
  • ISO Tank and Bulk: To supply large-scale requirements of petrochemical complexes.

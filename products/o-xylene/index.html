O-Xylene: Key Aromatic Isomer for Phthalic Anhydride Production

HS Code: 290241

O-Xylene is one of the important isomers of xylene and a liquid aromatic hydrocarbon with the chemical formula C8H10. It is recognized as a vital building block in the chemical and petrochemical industries. At SATCO, o-xylene is supplied with the highest purity standards and in compliance with downstream industry requirements.

Grades and Product Variety

  • Petrochemical/Synthesis Grade: High purity, suitable for catalytic oxidation and the production of valuable derivatives.

Key Advantages

  • High Purity: Minimum isomeric impurities for maximum efficiency in chemical processes.
  • Excellent Reactivity: Suitable structure for oxidation reactions and the formation of dicarboxylic ring compounds.
  • Optimal Solvency: Effective performance as a solvent in specialized industrial formulations.

Primary Applications

  • Phthalic Anhydride Production: Primary raw material for producing PVC plasticizers (DOP), polyester resins, and alkyd resins.
  • Paint and Resin Industries: Used as a solvent in the manufacturing of industrial paints, coatings, and varnishes.
  • Chemical Production: Utilization in the synthesis of insecticides, pharmaceuticals, and fragrance compounds.

Packaging and Supply

  • Specialized Metal Drums (200L): Standard and durable for secure storage and transport.
  • ISO Tank and Bulk: Suitable for delivery to large consuming complexes.

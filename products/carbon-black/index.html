Carbon Black: A Key Reinforcing Agent in Rubber and Coating Industries

HS Code: 280300

Carbon Black is one of the most essential industrial additives, widely used as a reinforcing filler and pigment in a vast range of products. At SATCO, this product is supplied with the highest quality standards to enhance the physical and mechanical properties of industrial and commercial applications.

Grades and Product Variety

  • Rubber Grade: Featuring an optimized surface area and structure, designed to increase wear resistance, tensile strength, and durability in rubber products (such as tires).
  • Specialty Grade: Featuring high purity and uniform particle distribution, suitable for use in inks, plastic masterbatches, coatings, and protective layers.

Key Advantages

  • Durability Enhancement: Significant improvement in wear and tear resistance for polymer compounds.
  • Thermal Stability: Excellent resistance to heat and environmental factors for end-user products.
  • High Pigmentation: Exceptional covering power and production of deep, consistent black tones.
  • Safety Standards: Compliant with strict quality control principles in packaging and transport.

Primary Applications

  • Tire and Rubber Industry: Used as the primary reinforcing filler in the production of tires, hoses, conveyor belts, and automotive rubber parts.
  • Plastic Industry: Production of black masterbatches, polyethylene pipes, and packaging films for UV radiation protection.
  • Ink and Coating Industry: Production of high-quality printing inks and industrial paints resistant to atmospheric conditions.
  • Protective Coatings: Used as a black pigment in protective and anti-corrosion coatings.

Packaging and Supply

  • 25 kg Bags: Standard packaging for easy transport and precise formulation control.
  • Big Bags (Jumbo Bags): Designed for bulk supply and large-scale industrial production lines.

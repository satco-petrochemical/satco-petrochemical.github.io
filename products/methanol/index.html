Methanol: A Vital Raw Material for the Chemical Industry

HS Code: 290511

Methanol, also known as methyl alcohol, is the simplest alcohol and one of the most widely used basic chemicals in the industry. At SATCO, methanol is supplied with high purity for various industrial uses and as a solvent or raw material in chemical synthesis.

Grades and Product Variety

  • Industrial Grade: Featuring extremely high purity, suitable for petrochemical production such as formaldehyde and acetic acid.
  • Fuel Grade: Featuring optimized characteristics for use as a clean fuel and an octane booster.

Key Advantages

  • High Purity: Minimum impurities to ensure optimal performance in chemical reactions.
  • Excellent Solubility: Superior ability to dissolve organic and inorganic compounds.
  • Desired Reactivity: An ideal raw material for producing a wide chain of petrochemical derivatives.
  • Stability: Proper safety and stability under standard storage conditions.

Primary Applications

  • Chemical Production: Production of formalin, formaldehyde resins, and industrial solvents.
  • Fuel Industry: Gasoline additive to increase octane rating and reduce emissions.
  • Biodiesel Production: A fundamental role in the transesterification process for producing biofuels.
  • Textile and Paint Industry: Solvent for producing dyes, inks, and synthetic fibers.

Packaging and Supply

  • Standard Barrels (200 Liters): For safe transport and medium-scale use.
  • ISO Tank and Bulk: To supply large industrial and petrochemical needs.

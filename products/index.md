  <h1>Granular Urea: The Global Standard for Nitrogen Fertilizer and Industrial Feedstock</h1>
  <p><b>HS Code:</b> 310210</p>
  <p>Granular Urea is the premier nitrogen fertilizer, recognized globally for its high nutrient concentration and physical durability. Produced through the synthesis of ammonia and carbon dioxide, our Granular Urea is engineered for optimal release rates and minimal dusting during handling. Its spherical, uniform granule structure ensures consistent application, making it the preferred choice for both large-scale agricultural operations and complex industrial applications.</p>

  <h2>Key Features & Advantages</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><b>High Nutrient Concentration:</b> Boasts a guaranteed minimum nitrogen content of 46%, maximizing nutrient delivery per kilogram applied.</li>
    <li><b>Superior Physical Strength:</b> High crush resistance minimizes fines and dusting during bulk storage, pneumatic transfer, and mechanical spreading.</li>
    <li><b>Optimum Granule Size:</b> Uniform sizing (typically 2.0–4.0 mm) guarantees even distribution across wide spans, preventing striping in fields.</li>
    <li><b>Low Biuret Content:</b> Meticulously manufactured to maintain biuret levels well below strict agronomic thresholds, preventing foliage burn.</li>
    <li><b>Excellent Storage Stability:</b> Treated with high-quality anti-caking agents to maintain free-flowing properties even in humid climates.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Primary Applications</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><b>Agriculture & Horticulture:</b> Applied as a direct-broadcast soil fertilizer or integrated into custom NPK bulk blends for cereal, grain, fruit, and vegetable crops.</li>
    <li><b>Industrial & Manufacturing:</b> Utilized as a key chemical feedstock in the production of resins, adhesives (urea-formaldehyde), plastics, and glues.</li>
    <li><b>Environmental & Emission Control:</b> Essential component in Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF / AdBlue) and selective catalytic reduction (SCR) systems to drastically reduce NOx emissions from diesel engines.</li>
    <li><b>Animal Feed Supplement:</b> Used under controlled conditions as a non-protein nitrogen (NPN) source in ruminant livestock feed formulations.</li>
  </ul>

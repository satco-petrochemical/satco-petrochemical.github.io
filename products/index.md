  <h1 style="color: #0d1b2a; margin-top: 0;">Granular Urea: The Global Standard for Nitrogen Fertilizer and Industrial Feedstock[cite: 1]</h1>
  <p><b>HS Code:</b> 310210[cite: 1]</p>
  <p>Granular Urea is the premier nitrogenous fertilizer, recognized globally for its high nutrient concentration and physical durability. Produced through the synthesis of ammonia and carbon dioxide, our Granular Urea is engineered for optimal release rates and minimal dusting during handling. Its spherical, uniform granule structure ensures consistent application, making it the preferred choice for both large-scale agricultural operations and complex industrial applications.[cite: 1]</p>

  <h2 style="color: #0d1b2a; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 5px; margin-top: 25px;">Product Grades & Variations:</h2>
  <p>At SATCO, we recognize that different industries require specific physical and chemical properties. Our Granular Urea is available in the following variations to meet diverse engineering and agricultural demands:[cite: 1]</p>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8;">
    <li><b>Agricultural Grade (Standard):</b> Optimized for broad-acre farming, featuring high crushing strength for pneumatic spreading and consistent nutrient release.[cite: 1]</li>
    <li><b>Industrial Grade (Low Biuret):</b> Specifically processed to maintain ultra-low biuret levels, making it the ideal feedstock for high-performance resins, adhesives, and chemical synthesis.[cite: 1]</li>
    <li><b>Technical Grade:</b> Designed for specialized chemical applications where strict impurity limits are required to prevent interference with downstream catalytic processes.[cite: 1]</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 style="color: #0d1b2a; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 5px; margin-top: 25px;">Key Advantages:</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8;">
    <li><b>High Nutrient Density:</b> Contains 46% nitrogen by weight, providing maximum fertilization efficiency per unit.[cite: 1]</li>
    <li><b>Superior Physical Integrity:</b> Enhanced mechanical strength minimizes degradation during shipping and pneumatic spreading.[cite: 1]</li>
    <li><b>Excellent Handling:</b> Uniform grain size reduces bridging in silos and ensures precise flow in mechanical spreaders.[cite: 1]</li>
    <li><b>Controlled Release:</b> Engineered for optimal dissolution rates, reducing nitrogen leaching in various soil types.[cite: 1]</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 style="color: #0d1b2a; border-bottom: 2px solid #d4af37; padding-bottom: 5px; margin-top: 25px;">Primary Applications:</h2>
  <ul style="line-height: 1.8;">
    <li><b>Agriculture:</b> The fundamental nitrogen source for cereals, fruits, and vegetables globally.[cite: 1]</li>
    <li><b>Industrial Resin Production:</b> A vital raw material for the production of urea-formaldehyde (UF) and melamine-formaldehyde (MF) resins.[cite: 1]</li>
    <li><b>Chemical Feedstock:</b> Used in the manufacturing of various industrial chemicals and adhesives.[cite: 1]</li>
    <li><b>Animal Feed Supplements:</b> Utilized in high-grade ruminant feed formulations under strict quality control.[cite: 1]</li>
  </ul>

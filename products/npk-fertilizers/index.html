NPK Fertilizers: Comprehensive Plant Nutrition Formulations

HS Code: 310520

NPK fertilizers are versatile products that deliver three vital elements—nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium—in a balanced composition. These fertilizers are designed to meet the nutritional requirements of plants at various growth stages, from germination to fruit maturation[cite: 5]. SATCO supplies high-grade NPK fertilizers with precise formulations to enhance productivity in modern agriculture[cite: 5].

Product Grades & Variations

SATCO ensures the supply of NPK in diverse and optimized ratios for various plant requirements[cite: 5]:

  • Balanced Formulations (e.g., 20-20-20): Suitable for general plant strengthening throughout the growth season[cite: 5].
  • Specialized Formulations: Custom ratios tailored for root development (high phosphorus), vegetative growth (high nitrogen), or enhancing crop quality and stress resistance (high potassium)[cite: 5].

Key Advantages

  • Complete Nutrition: Provides all three primary elements simultaneously to prevent any nutritional deficiencies in plants[cite: 5].
  • High Efficiency: Formulated for excellent solubility and rapid uptake by both roots and leaves[cite: 5].
  • Consistent Quality: Produced using high-purity raw materials to ensure the health of the final crop[cite: 5].
  • Supply Chain Safety: Rigorous adherence to international standards for the specialized storage and transport of compound fertilizers[cite: 5].

Primary Applications

  • Arable Crops: Increases quantitative and qualitative yield in cereals, oilseeds, and industrial plants[cite: 5].
  • Horticulture & Greenhouses: Specialized nutrition for fruit trees, ornamental flowers, and greenhouse crops with controlled ratios[cite: 5].
  • Precision Agriculture: Suitable for drip irrigation systems (fertigation) to deliver nutrients directly to the root zone[cite: 5].
  • Turf & Landscaping: Strengthens urban turf and landscape plants to maintain vitality and color year-round[cite: 5].

Packaging & Supply

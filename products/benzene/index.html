Benzene: Fundamental Aromatic Hydrocarbon in Petrochemical Industries

HS Code: 290220

Benzene is an aromatic hydrocarbon and clear liquid with the chemical formula C6H6, recognized as one of the most vital foundational raw materials in the petrochemical industry for producing a wide range of intermediate chemicals and polymers[cite: 5]. At SATCO, benzene is supplied with the highest levels of purity and refinery-grade standards[cite: 5].

Grades and Product Variety

  • Petrochemical Grade: High purity, suitable for advanced chemical syntheses and the production of benzene derivatives[cite: 5].

Key Advantages

  • High Chemical Purity: Minimum impurities, including sulfur and non-aromatic compounds, to guarantee the quality of subsequent reactions[cite: 5].
  • Stable Structure: A stable aromatic ring with optimal reactivity in electrophilic substitution reactions[cite: 5].
  • Excellent Solubility: Suitable solvent properties for various compounds and a key role as a building block[cite: 5].

Primary Applications

  • Ethylbenzene Production: The primary raw material for producing styrene monomer and ultimately styrenic plastics[cite: 5].
  • Cumene Production: A key intermediate for producing phenol and acetone[cite: 5].
  • Cyclohexane Production: The raw material for manufacturing nylon fibers and caprolactam[cite: 5].
  • Chemical and Pharmaceutical Industries: Synthesis of various dyes, detergents, and pharmaceutical compounds[cite: 5].

Packaging and Supply

  • Specialized Metal Drums (200L): Durable and safe for aromatic chemicals[cite: 5].
  • ISO Tank and Bulk: To supply the large-scale demands of downstream petrochemical industries[cite: 5].

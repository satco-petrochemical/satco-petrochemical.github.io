Basic Chemicals

Methanol

Essential chemical building block utilized widely in the production of formaldehyde, acetic acid, and various downstream petrochemical derivatives.

Ammonia

Vital industrial compound primarily used as a foundational feedstock for fertilizers, explosives, and diverse nitrogen-containing chemicals.

Caustic Soda (Sodium Hydroxide)

Strong strong alkali chemical heavily deployed in paper manufacturing, water treatment, alumina production, and soap-making processes.

Ethylene Dichloride (EDC)

Chlorinated hydrocarbon predominantly used as the primary intermediate precursor for manufacturing Vinyl Chloride Monomer (VCM) and PVC.

Vinyl Chloride Monomer (VCM)

Gaseous organochlorine compound utilized almost exclusively for the polymerization and mass production of polyvinyl chloride (PVC) plastics.

Sulfuric Acid

Highly corrosive mineral acid crucial for fertilizer manufacturing, mineral processing, oil refining, and extensive wastewater treatment operations.

Specialty Polymers

Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene (ABS)

Terpolymer with high impact resistance and toughness, extensively used in automotive components, electronics, and protective helmets.

Thermoplastic Elastomers (TPE)

Copolymers combining thermoplastic and elastomeric properties, ideal for soft-touch grips, automotive seals, and medical devices.

Polycarbonate (PC)

Strong, durable, and transparent thermoplastic offering high thermal resistance and exceptional impact strength for lenses and safety equipment.

Polyamide / Nylon (PA)

Engineering plastic featuring outstanding mechanical strength, wear resistance, and chemical stability for gears and industrial applications.

Ethylene Vinyl Acetate (EVA)

Elastomeric polymer that produces rubber-like flexibility, commonly used in footwear soles, foam padding, and photovoltaic encapsulation.

Polyoxymethylene / Acetal (POM)

Engineering thermoplastic characterized by high stiffness, low friction, and excellent dimensional stability for precision parts.

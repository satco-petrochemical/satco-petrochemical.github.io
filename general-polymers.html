General Polymers

High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE)

High-strength thermoplastic polymer widely used in blow molding, pipe extrusion, and injection molding applications.

Low-Density Polyethylene (LDPE)

Flexible polymer with unique flow properties, ideal for film packaging, sheets, and various extrusion coatings.

Linear Low-Density Polyethylene (LLDPE)

Substantially linear polymer featuring significant numbers of short branches, widely used for stretch films and general packaging.

Polypropylene Raffia (PP Raffia)

High-tenacity grade designed for woven sacks, carpet backing, ropes, and flexible intermediate bulk containers (FIBC).

Polypropylene Chemical (PP Chemical)

Injection molding and specialized chemical grade offering excellent balance of mechanical properties and processability.

Polyvinyl Chloride (PVC)

Versatile synthetic plastic polymer extensively utilized in construction for pipes, window profiles, and wire insulation.

General Purpose Polystyrene (GPPS)

Rigid, transparent thermoplastic homopolymer used for consumer electronics housings, disposable cutlery, and packaging.

High Impact Polystyrene (HIPS)

Rubber-modified polystyrene offering high impact strength, excellent formability, and durability for appliance parts.

Polyethylene Terephthalate (PET)

High-clarity thermoplastic polyester resin widely applied in beverage bottles, synthetic fibers, and food packaging containers.
